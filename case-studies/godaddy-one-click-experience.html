GoDaddy One-click Experience

UX Design, 2025

Challenge

SEO setup was a major drop-off point for GoDaddy Website + Marketing customers. Most small business owners don't know what a keyword, meta title, or search description is, let alone how to write good ones, and asking them to create that content from scratch was costing completions. User testing confirmed the friction directly. Contributors called terms like "keywords," "search title," and "search description" unclear, and several couldn't tell what the "Publish Now" button would actually do.

Solution

Rather than a blank-slate content generator, SEO 1-Click pre-fills AI-generated SEO recommendations pulled directly from the customer's own published content, and asks for a single review-and-approve action instead of manual authoring. User research showed the underlying insight, that customers trust AI far more when it's working with content they already published than when it's inventing something from nothing, so the flow was built entirely around that trust.

Success

  • Setup completion rate rose from 56% to 73% (+17 points).
  • Time-to-complete dropped from 7m 18s to 2m 14s, 69% faster.
  • $1.88M in annual revenue impact ($318 revenue per monthly engaged user).
  • 82.5% of users published immediately after optimization.
