Challenge

Most GoDaddy Website + Marketing customers know they should be doing more marketing, such as SEO, social posts, and email campaigns, but don't have the time, skill, or confidence to do it themselves. DIFY ("Done-For-You") exists to close that gap, surfacing AI-driven marketing actions customers can approve rather than build from scratch, across an ecosystem that spans the Marketing Calendar, evergreen email campaigns, and Airo.ai-powered experiences.

Solution

As part of GoDaddy's Discover Marketing team, I support the UX behind these done-for-you experiences, including the Calendar tab that expanded marketing content discovery, email recommendations layered onto Calendar to strengthen it as a one-stop shop for content, and the ongoing refinement of Airo.ai-powered marketing agents as they move from experiment to production. Each surface is iterated through the same loop of release, A/B test, read the lift, and refine, rather than shipped once and left alone.

Success

  • DIFY conversions up 57% versus target in a single quarter, with leads up 224% year-over-year.
  • DIFY lead-to-SQL conversion rate improved to 15.69%, against a goal of 11.98%.
  • The Calendar tab rollout drove a +19.55% page-view lift, and email recommendations on Calendar added a further +13% lift in email campaigns created.
  • Paid Website + Marketing monthly active marketers grew 12.6% year-over-year, exceeding a 12% goal.
