Core Card

Core Card App

UX/UI Design

Challenge

A U.S. fintech client needed a credit card program built from the ground up — a rewards card for a values-driven niche market, offering cashback and charitable giving with every purchase. The design process had to move fast enough to hit an aggressive MVP timeline, even after the project lost its technical lead and QA lead early on.

Solution

I led UX and UI design for the iOS-native card app: wireframes for account sign-up, credit decisioning, transaction history, bill pay, and rewards, validated with the client every sprint through a structured two-week review cycle — a UX demo and comment period, followed by a UI demo and final approval — before handoff to development. The result was a clean, trustworthy mobile experience built around 1% unlimited cashback, full Visa benefits, and $0 annual, monthly, and foreign transaction fees.

Success

Despite early turnover on the client's core team, the MVP shipped on schedule for the U.S. market. The two-week design-approval cadence kept a 25-person cross-discipline team — UX, UI, business analysis, development, and QA — aligned sprint over sprint, and a simple comment-complexity framework let most design feedback get resolved in-house instead of stalling on the client. Post-launch usage data wasn't part of this engagement's scope, so the wins here are process and delivery ones, not user-growth metrics.
