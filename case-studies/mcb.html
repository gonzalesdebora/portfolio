MCB

Poster Design, 2015

Challenge

The Museu da Casa Brasileira runs an annual open poster competition tied to its Design Prize, one of Brazil's most established design awards, inviting designers to interpret the prize and the museum's identity in a single image, with no other brief than the theme itself.

Solution

I designed a poster submission for the competition's open call, working within the museum's own institutional identity while finding a distinct visual angle to stand out among the pool of national entries.

Success

The poster was submitted to the Museu da Casa Brasileira's Design Prize competition, part of a broader body of self-directed poster work made for open design calls.
