Challenge

Grameen America’s model runs on trust — members manage microloans through weekly in-person meetings. But that meant no way to check a loan balance, make a payment, or apply for a new loan outside of a meeting or a phone call, which ate up huge amounts of branch staff time.

Solution

We designed My Grameen, a bilingual mobile app, as a digital front door to the program. The home screen puts loan status front and center with a one-tap "Pay Now," the loan application became a short guided flow instead of paper, and branch staff got their own real-time view of repayment and attendance across their group.

Success

Registration hit 98% of members, 85% of loan applications now happen in-app, and the app holds a Net Promoter Score of 88 with over 20,000 reviews.
