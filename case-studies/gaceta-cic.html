Gaceta CIC

Illustration for an Academic Research Gazette

Illustration

Challenge

Gaceta CIC is the yearly publication of Universidad de los Andes' Centro de Investigación y Creación, covering research grants, ethics protocols, and a dossier of academic essays — dense institutional content that still needed a visual presence to keep it from reading like a pure administrative report.

Solution

I contributed illustration work to Issue No. 3 (2017), joining a small group of artists commissioned to punctuate the gazette's sections — from the call-for-proposals listings to the dossier on the study and preservation of Colombian cultural heritage — with imagery that gave the academic content room to breathe.

Success

The completed issue (ISSN 2539-2964) shipped as a full editorial publication for the Centro de Investigación y Creación, blending institutional reporting with contributed art across its sections.
